Results

Every alert, settled

Every alert we send is recorded at the Aussie price it went out at, then settled from official box scores once the game ends. Wins, losses, pushes and voids all go in. Alerts still waiting on their game don't appear until they're settled.

Closing value compares each alerted Aussie price with the US market’s no-vig price at the last check before the game: the alerted price × the US chance − 1. Above zero means the alert beat where the US market closed. Every alert sent counts once its game has started, voided bets aside. It measures price, not profit: you can still lose. After each alert, the Aussie price is checked again at each scan at the same bookmaker and line, until it drops or the game starts. A drop is timed halfway between the last check that found the old price and the first that didn’t. Timings are based on 100 alerts followed from their first check. Kick-off figures use the last check before each game. A drop doesn’t mean the alert won, only that the old price didn’t last.

Questions

Straight answers

Is this a tipping service?

Not in the usual sense. Odds Ace doesn't predict results or promise wins. Each alert flags an Aussie price that hasn't caught up with the US market, and you decide whether to bet.

Why do Aussie bookies follow the American market?

Far more is bet on these sports in America, so prices there move first. Australian bookmakers adjust after them, and for a short time some Aussie prices still show the old line.

When does Odds Ace scan?

Around the clock: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. When a gap shows up, it's re-checked and then posted to the Telegram channel. Prices move fast, so a price may have changed by the time you look.

Which bookmakers do you compare?

These 7 Australian-licensed bookmakers: TAB, Sportsbet, Neds, PointsBet, Unibet, Betr and Betfair. We compare them against 8 major American sportsbooks, which we don't name because they aren't licensed in Australia. Odds Ace isn't affiliated with or paid by any bookmaker.

Do I need an American betting account?

No. You bet with your own Australian bookmaker, as you normally would. The American prices are only used as a reference.

How do alerts arrive?

In a private Telegram channel. Turn on notifications if you want each alert straight away, or mute the channel whenever you want a break, such as overnight.

How do I get into the Telegram channel?

Within 5 minutes of starting your free trial, we email you a personal invite link. Open it in Telegram and tap Request to Join, and you'll be let in automatically within a minute or two. The link is just for you, so please don't share it. If your membership ends, you're taken out of the channel.

How do I pause or stop alerts?

To pause, mute the Odds Ace channel in Telegram (tap Mute at the bottom of the channel) and unmute whenever you like. To stop, cancel your membership (see How do I cancel?) or leave the channel. If you want a break from gambling or have self-excluded, email us and we'll cancel straight away and refund the unused part of your month.

Can bookmakers limit my account?

Yes. Australian bookmakers can limit accounts that keep taking their slow prices, and can void bets at prices they treat as an obvious error, even after accepting them.
